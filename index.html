My favourite event in Sweden-Midsommar

Midsommar is one of the most popular celebrations in Sweden. It is celebrated in June, when the days are long and bright. People decorate a maypole with flowers and leaves, dance around it, and spend time with family and friends. Traditional foods include potatoes, herring, strawberries, and cream. Midsummer is a joyful celebration of summer and nature.
